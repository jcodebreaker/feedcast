It writes a short note about who you are, and that note is all the model sees of you

Seeded from git, session titles and your own LinkedIn profile, compressed by one Haiku call into a file you can open and edit. No project names. Your edits survive the nightly refresh.

Read on your Mac

  • Repo names and the first README paragraph
  • Dependency names (package.json, pyproject.toml)
  • Last 30 commit subjects per repo
  • Claude Code session titles and the first prompt, cut to 200 characters
  • Your LinkedIn headline, About, roles and skills (feedcast profile --linkedin, once)
  • Your pins and mutes

Never leaves your Mac: source code, diffs, full transcripts, Chrome cookies.

It pulls out what people shared, then checks each post against your work
